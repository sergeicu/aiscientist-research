# Research Task: Comprehensive Breadth Analysis of AI Research Assistant Companies

## Objective
Conduct comprehensive research to identify 20-30 companies/projects building AI tools that use LLMs to accelerate general research tasks. Focus on breadth across multiple categories.

## Context
We're surveying the landscape of AI-powered research assistance tools. We want to find companies using LLMs for general research acceleration - NOT specialized models for specific scientific tasks (like protein folding, drug discovery models, etc.).

## What We're Looking For
Companies/projects using LLMs to help with:
- Literature research and discovery
- Surveying state of the art
- Creating hypotheses
- Summarizing experiments
- Question answering from research papers
- Research workflow automation
- Knowledge synthesis
- Paper writing assistance
- Research ideation

## Known Examples (for reference)
- lila.ai
- edison scientific (Future House spinoff)
- leap labs
- sakana.ai
- undermind (YC company)
- answerthis.io
- sciloop (YC company)

## Inclusion Criteria
- Early stage companies (include)
- Any research domain (biomedical, CS, physics, general, etc.)
- Open source projects (include)
- Both funded startups and emerging tools
- Commercial and non-commercial projects

## Exclusion Criteria
- Specialized models for specific tasks (AlphaFold, protein folding, drug discovery models)
- General lab management software without AI
- Traditional reference managers without LLM features
- Pure data analysis tools without research context

## Research Approach
Search across these sources:
1. YCombinator company directory (AI/research filters)
2. Product Hunt (research tools, AI tools)
3. Recent venture funding announcements (AI research tools)
4. GitHub trending/popular (research automation)
5. Academic tool lists and aggregators
6. Tech news sites (TechCrunch, VentureBeat, etc.)
7. AI research Twitter/X discussions
8. arXiv papers about research automation tools

## Categories to Cover
Aim to find companies across these categories:
1. **Literature Discovery & Search** - semantic search, paper recommendations
2. **Full Research Automation** - end-to-end AI scientists
3. **Question Answering** - research question answering systems
4. **Hypothesis Generation** - AI-assisted ideation
5. **Research Summarization** - paper summaries, experiment summaries
6. **Knowledge Synthesis** - connecting ideas across papers
7. **Research Writing** - AI-assisted paper writing
8. **Experiment Planning** - research design assistance
9. **Citation & Reference Management** - AI-enhanced reference tools
10. **Multi-modal Research** - tools handling papers, data, code together

## Output Format
For each company/project provide:
- **Name**
- **URL** (website, GitHub, or best available link)
- **Category** (from above or new if needed)
- **Brief Description** (1-2 sentences on what they do)
- **Stage** (early-stage startup / funded / open-source / research project)
- **Key Feature** (one standout capability)

Group results by category. Aim for 20-30 total entries.

## What NOT to Include
- Business model analysis
- Pricing information
- Detailed competitive analysis
- Team/funding details (unless readily available and interesting)

## Deliverable
A markdown report with:
1. Executive summary (key trends you notice)
2. Companies organized by category
3. Any interesting patterns or gaps you observe
4. Total count of companies found

Focus on discovering and categorizing as many relevant companies as possible.
