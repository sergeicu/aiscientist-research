# Research Task: AI Research Tools Market Landscape (Including Adjacent Spaces)

## Objective
Create a broad market landscape view of the "AI for research" ecosystem. Include adjacent spaces and tools that intersect with AI-powered research assistance. Identify patterns, clusters, and gaps in the market.

## Context
We want a holistic view of companies building in and around the AI research assistance space. This includes core AI research tools AND adjacent categories that are incorporating LLM capabilities or could be considered part of the broader ecosystem.

## What We're Looking For
PRIMARY: Companies using LLMs for general research acceleration
ADJACENT: Tools that intersect with or complement AI research assistance

## Known Core Examples (for reference)
- lila.ai
- edison scientific (Future House spinoff)
- leap labs
- sakana.ai
- undermind (YC company)
- answerthis.io
- sciloop (YC company)

## Market Segments to Explore

### Core AI Research Assistance
Direct competitors/peers to the examples above:
- Literature search and discovery
- Research automation
- Question answering
- Hypothesis generation
- Experiment summarization
- Full AI scientists

### Adjacent Space 1: Enhanced Reference Managers
Traditional reference managers adding AI/LLM features:
- Citation management with AI
- Paper organization with semantic search
- AI-powered paper recommendations
- Smart tagging and categorization
- Examples to find more like: Zotero plugins, Mendeley competitors

### Adjacent Space 2: Research Collaboration & Communication
Tools for research teams incorporating AI:
- Collaborative research platforms
- Research knowledge bases
- Team research assistants
- Lab wikis with AI
- Research communication tools

### Adjacent Space 3: Smart Lab Notebooks & Documentation
Digital lab notebooks with AI capabilities:
- AI-assisted documentation
- Experiment tracking with insights
- Protocol generation
- Results interpretation
- Examples might include: Benchling competitors, ELN with AI

### Adjacent Space 4: Academic Writing Assistants
AI writing tools focused on research/academic writing:
- Research paper writing
- Academic language improvement
- Literature synthesis
- Citation assistance
- Outline generation for research papers

### Adjacent Space 5: Data Analysis & Visualization with AI Guidance
Tools helping researchers analyze and visualize data using LLMs:
- AI-guided statistical analysis
- Natural language data queries
- Automated visualization suggestions
- Interpretation assistance
- NOT specialized models, but LLM-guided analysis

### Adjacent Space 6: Research Discovery & Trend Analysis
Tools for understanding research trends and emerging topics:
- Research trend tracking
- Emerging topic identification
- Field mapping
- Researcher discovery
- Grant opportunity matching

### Adjacent Space 7: Knowledge Management for Researchers
Personal knowledge management tools for researchers with AI:
- Research note-taking with AI
- Knowledge graphs for research
- Connected note systems
- Second brain for researchers

### Adjacent Space 8: Open Source Research Automation
Open source projects and frameworks:
- Research automation frameworks
- LLM research agent projects
- Academic tool libraries
- Community-driven research tools

### Adjacent Space 9: Research Education & Methodology
Tools teaching research methods or assisting learning:
- Research methodology assistants
- Statistics tutors for researchers
- Protocol databases with AI search
- Research training platforms

## Inclusion Criteria
- Early stage to established
- Any research domain
- Open source projects (definitely include)
- Both pure-play and tools adding AI features
- International companies (not just US)
- Academic projects that might become commercial
- Stealth mode startups if discoverable

## Research Sources
1. YCombinator, Techstars, other accelerators
2. Product Hunt (multiple categories)
3. GitHub trending and popular repos
4. Academic software directories
5. University tech transfer offices
6. Research software repositories
7. Venture capital portfolio pages (AI research focused)
8. Academic conference tool demos
9. Research communities (Reddit, Discord, Slack)
10. App stores (academic/research categories)

## Output Format
For each company/project:
- **Name**
- **URL**
- **Market Segment** (Core or which Adjacent Space)
- **Description** (1-2 sentences)
- **Stage** (startup/funded/open-source/established)
- **Distinguishing Feature**
- **Adjacent or Core** (flag it)

## Deliverable Structure
1. **Executive Summary**
   - Market map overview
   - Key patterns observed
   - Crowded vs. sparse segments
   - Interesting gaps or opportunities

2. **Core AI Research Assistance Companies**
   - Full list with details

3. **Adjacent Spaces** (for each)
   - Companies in that space
   - How they intersect with core research assistance
   - Maturity of AI integration

4. **Market Observations**
   - Convergence trends
   - Technology patterns
   - Geographic distribution
   - Stage distribution
   - Open source vs. commercial balance

5. **Gaps & Opportunities**
   - Underserved areas
   - Potential whitespace

## What NOT to Include
- Deep business analysis
- Pricing strategies
- Revenue/funding details (unless very notable)
- Specialized scientific models (protein folding, etc.)
- Pure database/infrastructure without AI features

## Goal
Create the most comprehensive view of the ecosystem. Cast a wide net. Include 30-50+ companies/projects total across core and adjacent spaces. Help understand where the market is heading and where opportunities exist.
