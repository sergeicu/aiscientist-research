# Research Task: AI Research Tools Segmented by Research Workflow Stage

## Objective
Map AI research assistant companies/projects to specific stages of the research workflow. Find 3-5+ companies per stage to understand which parts of the research process are being addressed by AI tools.

## Context
We're surveying companies using LLMs to accelerate research. Instead of broad categorization, organize findings by where they fit in the research lifecycle. This will help identify which workflow stages are crowded vs. underserved.

## What We're Looking For
Companies/projects using LLMs to help with general research tasks (NOT specialized models for protein folding, drug discovery, etc.). We want to understand which stage(s) of research they address.

## Known Examples (for reference)
- lila.ai
- edison scientific (Future House spinoff)
- leap labs
- sakana.ai
- undermind (YC company)
- answerthis.io
- sciloop (YC company)

Find 3-5+ companies addressing this stage.

Find 3-5+ companies addressing this stage.

Find 3-5+ companies addressing this stage.

### Stage 4: Execution & Data Collection

### Stage 5: Analysis & Interpretation

## Inclusion Criteria
- Early stage companies (include)
- Any research domain (biomedical, CS, physics, general, etc.)
- Open source projects (include)
- Both commercial and non-commercial
- Tools that may span multiple stages (note all stages they address)

## Research Sources
1. YCombinator companies
2. Product Hunt
3. GitHub (research automation, AI research tools)
4. Academic tool directories
5. Research software registries
6. Tech news and blogs
7. AI/ML research communities
8. University innovation/spinoff lists

## Output Format
For each company/project provide:
- **Name**
- **URL**
- **Primary Stage(s)** (which workflow stage(s) they address)
- **Brief Description** (1-2 sentences)
- **Stage** (early-stage / funded / open-source / research project)
- **Key Capability** (specific to their workflow stage)

## Deliverable Structure
Organize by workflow stage:
1. Summary of findings (which stages are crowded, which are gaps)
2. For each workflow stage:
   - List of companies addressing it
   - Company details as specified above
3. Companies addressing multiple stages
4. Observations about the distribution across the research lifecycle

## What NOT to Include
- Pricing or business models
- Detailed competitive analysis
- Financial information
- Specialized scientific models (AlphaFold-like tools)

Focus on discovering tools and mapping them to research workflow stages to understand the landscape.
